Where the numbers come from

County vote counts come from the Texas Secretary of State's unofficial election-night results, checked against Associated Press counts reported by Decision Desk HQ and news outlets. A solid ✓ AP badge means the Associated Press has called the race.

Counties usually post early and mail votes first, around 7 p.m., then Election Day votes through the night. Early votes lean more Democratic than Election Day votes, so leads often shrink as the night goes on.

How the live model works

Each county's expected turnout is its 2024 turnout scaled to the statewide 2026 projection. Votes not yet counted in a county are estimated from that county's pre-election projection, blended with its own Election Day results once those come in. The statewide total of counted plus estimated votes gives the projected margin; the uncertainty shrinks as more of the expected vote is counted.

A dashed ◆ badge is the Hilltop projection: shown only at 99%+ confidence with at least 80% counted, holding across two straight updates, and with prediction markets agreeing. It is not an AP call.
