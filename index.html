About these results

Vote counts come from Associated Press tabulations as reported by news outlets, and from the Texas Secretary of State. A candidate is shown as the winner only after the AP declares the race.

Polymarket odds update every 30 seconds and Kalshi odds about every five minutes. On election night, results update about every three to five minutes from the first poll closings at 5 p.m. Central. This page checks for new data every 30 seconds, so there is no need to reload.

How win chances work

Before votes are counted, the Hilltop forecast averages the Decision Desk HQ forecast (or the race rating) with Kalshi and Polymarket prices. As votes come in, the counted margin is blended with that forecast, weighted by how much of the expected vote is in. Control of each chamber is estimated from 4,000 simulated outcomes that share a national swing.
